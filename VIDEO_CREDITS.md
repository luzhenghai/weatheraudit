# Demo video credits

Final reviewed video: `weather_audit_demo_v4.mp4` (shared separately from this repository), 118.016 seconds, 1080p, 30 fps. The shorter filename `weather_audit_demo_v2.mp4` was updated to identical final content; the explicit v4 filename is preferred to avoid confusing old copies.

The video visualizes saved experimental results with authored animation and bilingual captions. It is not a live screen recording or evidence of an operational weather service. The opening temperature scenario is illustrative.

* Scientific inputs: the main notebook, `results_v4.json`, and its generated figures. References and dataset credits are in the README.
* Narration: synthetic English Andrew voice (Microsoft), produced with https://github.com/rany2/edge-tts .
* Visuals and mix: generated typography, animated tables and diagrams, synthesized music, rain and cue sounds produced for this project with AI assistance. No external music recording is credited in the production notes.
* Audio workflow reference: https://github.com/liuchong110/soundcheck . Its generic score is a production guide, not a scientific quality guarantee; the low-mid ratio target was not fully met, and no silent passage was available to measure a separate noise floor.
* AI assistance: OpenAI Codex assisted with video code, narration revisions, generated graphics/audio, mixing and factual review. The entrant directed the work. Project coding/drafting assistance from Claude and WorkBuddy is disclosed in the README.

The final video removes the unfair TimesFM comparison, limits the provenance claim to the four scored events, distinguishes unverified control events from known false alarms, specifies positive injections, and distinguishes lower mean errors from confidence intervals excluding zero.
